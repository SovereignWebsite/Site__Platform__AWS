AWS Platform Sovereign Website

Run your AWS accounts from this page, in your own browser. You sign in with Google; AWS gives this page credentials for one hour, for the roles you created; read-only unless you approve a plan. No AWS key is ever made, and nothing you sign in with leaves this tab except to Google and AWS.

1. Sign in with Google

The owner has not set this site's Google client ID yet (config.js).

Restored from this device's vault: your session was sealed with a key only this site, in this browser, can use.

Your Google account ID: . Not a secret; your AWS roles trust only this ID and this site.

2. Your AWS accounts

Account IDs are kept on this device only, sealed with its key; never on the page or in any repository.

    Setting up an account creates two roles with CloudFormation, once: sws-aws-platform-read (view only, plus budgets and costs) and sws-aws-platform-change (only the changes listed in the template). Both trust only your Google ID through this site, for one hour at a time.

    3. Plans waiting for your approval

    Each plan reads what AWS holds now, shows it beside what it will hold after, and changes nothing until you press Approve.

      How this works

      • Google gives this page an ID token for you. AWS STS (AssumeRoleWithWebIdentity) swaps it for credentials that end within the hour.
      • So a refresh does not sign you out, the page seals both with this device's own key before keeping them: a key pair made by your browser for this site, whose private half cannot be read by anyone, this page included; the browser only lets this site use it. Sealed records end when the credentials do. Sign out deletes them; Forget this device also deletes the key.
      • Your roles are the mandate: they are written as infrastructure as code, and a role can do only what its template lists.
      • Calls go from your browser straight to AWS (STS, IAM, Cost Explorer), signed in the page with your one-hour credentials. No server of ours sits in between, and there is no tracking.
