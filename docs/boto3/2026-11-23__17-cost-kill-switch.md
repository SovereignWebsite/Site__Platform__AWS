## Addendum, 18:20 UTC: the longest alarm period (for the daily requests alarm)

PutMetricAlarm: `Period` "Valid values are 10, 20, 30, and any multiple of 60"; "An alarm's total current evaluation period can be no longer than seven days, so Period multiplied by EvaluationPeriods can't be more than 604,800 seconds. For alarms with a period of less than one hour (3,600 seconds), the total evaluation period can't be longer than one day (86,400 seconds)." The CloudWatch user guide says the same: "The maximum evaluation period is seven days for alarms with a period of at least one hour (3600 seconds)." So `Period: 86400` (one day, a multiple of 60) with `EvaluationPeriods: 1` is allowed (86,400 is under 604,800); the 6 h fallback is not needed. Sum of CloudFront `Requests` over 86400 s is a valid statistic for it. A one-day period means the alarm can only judge a whole day's data, so it fires up to a day late by design: keep the faster alarm alongside it. Sources: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonCloudWatch/latest/APIReference/API_PutMetricAlarm.html; https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonCloudWatch/latest/monitoring/AlarmThatSendsEmail.html (read 23 Nov 2026).
